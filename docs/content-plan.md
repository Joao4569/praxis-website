# Bilingual content plan

The practice name is intentionally represented as “Praxis” / “Practice” until the final brand is
chosen. German (`de-CH`) is the primary language, and every public content page will have an
English equivalent.

## Therapist profile fields

- Name, pronouns if desired, professional title, and credentials
- Portrait and accessible alternative text
- Short introduction and therapeutic approach
- Client groups and areas of support
- Methods and specialisms
- Languages offered
- Practice days and current availability wording
- Billing and insurance information
- Direct telephone and secure email contact
- Education, clinical experience, and professional memberships
- Corresponding German and English copy review status

## Privacy and safety rules

- Do not ask visitors to describe sensitive medical details in an unprotected web form.
- Provide direct contact information for each therapist and clarify response expectations.
- Keep emergency information visible in the navigation but visually separate from routine contact.
- Do not use behavioural tracking, advertising pixels, or third-party embeds by default.
- Prefer a simple external map link over an automatically loaded tracking-heavy map embed.
